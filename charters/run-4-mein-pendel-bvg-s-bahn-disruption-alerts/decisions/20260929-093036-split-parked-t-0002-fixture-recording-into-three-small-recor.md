# Split parked T-0002 (fixture recording) into three small recording tasks and unblock the engine from the full fixture set

_Recorded 20260929-093036 by the NIGHTSHIFT planner._

## Context
T-0002 is parked because attempt 2 came back 'blocked' with no reason. Attempt 1 was sent back for revision: it counted an 'Additional service' status remark as a warning, invented a SEV on a bus line, and cut the journeys fixture short. T-0004 (the engine) depended on T-0002, and T-0005 and T-0009 depend on T-0004, so the whole G1 path and the G3 scheduler were stuck. The repo has no recorded fixtures yet, only the harness example. When the planner tried a single live curl to check reachability, the NIGHTSHIFT guard blocked it ('network tools are not available in the sandbox'). The planner did not try to get around this. Whether workers can reach the API is therefore unknown to the planner. Attempt 1 evidently did reach it. A likely cause of attempt 2's blocked report is that attempt 1's probing had already spent most of the ~30-request budget.

## Decision
Replace T-0002 with three sequential tasks, each with its own fresh, small live-call budget and exact-count reporting: T-0015 records the four core departures kinds that attempt 1 found real (undisturbed, cancellation, delay, construction), T-0016 records journeys (all legs, reaching the destination) and locations, and T-0017 records a rail Ersatzverkehr and a genuine warning, with strict rules on derivation. T-0004 now depends on T-0015 only. It implements all kinds, tests the recorded ones table-driven, and tests the rest on labelled synthetic inputs. New task T-0018 adds the T-0017 fixtures to the table and asserts coverage of at least five real kinds, which completes G1's definition of done. T-0005 now uses T-0016's fixture instead of recording its own. T-0004 and T-0005 have their fixture write access narrowed to their own subdirectories, and recorded files are now protected. Order: T-0013, T-0015, T-0004, T-0016, T-0005, T-0017, T-0018, then M3 (T-0006, T-0007, T-0008, T-0014, T-0009), then T-0010. This follows G1 > G3 > G2. Assumptions (conservative): every recording task stops and reports blocked, with the exact command and error, if the guard blocks the network, and never works around it. A kind that cannot be found is reported as a gap, never invented. The ~30-request cap from T-0002 is read as a per-task budget; the new budgets add up to 40 requests, spread across separate tasks and runs, which is well under the ~100 req/min limit.

## Consequences
The engine can land once the four core fixtures exist, even if the harder kinds (Ersatzverkehr, warning) are rare today. G3 tasks T-0006, T-0007 and T-0014 do not depend on any fixture, so work continues if recording is blocked. If workers also turn out to lack network access, T-0015 to T-0017 will come back blocked with a concrete error, and G1's definition of done will need a human decision on network access. That will be visible in the parked reasons. G4 and G5 are still unplanned until higher-priority work drains.
